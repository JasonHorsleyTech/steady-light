# Narrative Structure

## Four-Act Arc

### Act 1 / Book 1 — Small Town: "Don't Be an NPC"
**Core Belief:** Progress requires moving past the limits of others' knowledge, even when you feel unprepared.

You land in a small town with nothing. A farmer offers barn lodging if you clear his green slimes and do chores. The town has a weaponsmith, armorer, Fighters Guild, bank, bar, and a library nobody mentions.

Every NPC gives advice with conviction. All of it is correct — for fighting slimes. All of it is useless for anything else. The economy bleeds you dry while feeling like progress.

**Paradigm shifts in this act:**
- Stat flip: Calm/Confidence/Passion → Drive/Insight/Stability (via 3-night reflection arcs)
- Energy bar splits into three bars (Stage 1)
- Combat isn't turn-based — it's simultaneous rhythm-based (gradual realization)
- Gear/guild training is a treadmill, not real progression

**Ends when:** Player has enough Drive/Insight to leave town. They recognize the loop for what it is.

---

### Act 2 / Book 2 — The Wilds: "The Answer Is Not the Cabin"
**Core Belief:** Isolation and hoarding skills aren't the answer; real progress requires connection and adaptation.

First ventures outside town. Encounters outclass the slime-optimized loadout immediately. Gear breaks, food spoils, weather slows travel. You meet:
- Travelers with contradictory advice
- Fighters who mock your guild skills
- Merchants who price-gouge outsiders

The temptation to retreat to the barn is strong. Doing so halts growth.

**Paradigm shifts in this act:**
- Combat Stages 2-3: enemy intent visible, fights move to overworld
- Combat mastery > gear (progression paradigm shift fully realized)
- New time signatures in music/combat (3/4, 6/8)
- Self-reliance has limits — you need other people

**Ends when:** Player reaches the big city.

---

### Act 3 / Book 3 — Big City: "Confidence"
**Core Belief:** TBD — likely about social dynamics, relationships, and the difference between social confidence and actual understanding.

The gameplay shifts from combat to social dynamics. The city is about Confidence — navigating social hierarchies, forming alliances, helping NPCs with personal problems.

**Possible mechanics:**
- Dialogue becomes more complex. Once Confidence threshold is met, player can input their own dialogue (originally planned as GPT-driven — may now be Claude-driven or a constrained input system)
- Relationship stats: Familiarity, Trust, Impressed, Comfortability
- Social puzzles replace combat as primary challenge

**Paradigm shifts in this act:**
- TBD — likely about realizing social systems are as rigged as the small town economy

---

### Act 4 / Book 4 — Return: "Full Circle"
**Core Belief:** True progress means going back to make things better, not just escaping.

The player returns to the small town with new knowledge and skills. They can now see the systemic problems they couldn't see before and actually fix them.

**Key quests (from early brainstorm):**
- Crop rotation: convince farmers to change methods for better yield
- Cattle rancher: broker peace between rancher and farmers
- Shopkeeper: help expand inventory through trade relations

**Ends with:** Settling down, forming meaningful relationships, improving the town. The player's final goal is balancing personal happiness with community improvement.

## Writing Tone

- **Line length:** 4-18 words, present-tense preferred, concrete images
- **Reveal beats:** Third encounter with a system = the "click" moment
- **Avoid:** Lecturing, diagnostics, therapy-speak, overt stat names in dialogue
- **Use:** Sensory anchors ("air feels cooler"), physical verbs ("plant your feet")
- **NPC speech:** Conviction that reveals ignorance through specifics
- **Flip cadence:** Loss → questioning → reframing, then a short physical epilogue next morning

### Tone Templates
- After drink (progression ender): "Same as always. You take a sip and let it sit with you."
- Sleep after drink (loop): "Same as always. Out before your eyes can close."
- Morning after Drive flip: "You've got somewhere to be, and you start moving."
- Morning after Insight flip: "You see yourself in it — just one part of the whole."
- Morning after Stability flip: "You plant your feet before you rise."

## Characters & Relationships

### Relationship Stats (for NPCs)
- **Familiarity:** How comfortable the NPC is with you
- **Trust:** How much they trust you
- **Impressed:** How impressed they are with your abilities
- **Comfortability:** How at ease they are around you
