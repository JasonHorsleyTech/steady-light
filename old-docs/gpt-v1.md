# Game Design Document: [Working Title]

## Overview
This game is an RPG that focuses on personal growth, relationships, and navigating systems. The player starts in a small town, where they learn from NPCs who are often limited by their own ignorance and self-interest. As the player progresses, they gain insight, drive, and confidence to leave the town, battle through a mid-game of travel and exploration, and eventually reach a big city. In the city, the game shifts focus from combat to social dynamics and relationships. The endgame involves returning to the small town to solve its systemic issues, improve the quality of life, and form meaningful relationships, including marriage.

## Core Themes
- **Growth through experience**: Players learn to question what they are told and experiment with their own path.
- **Breaking out of NPC loops**: Players and NPCs are stuck in loops of behavior and need to break free by gaining insight, drive, or confidence.
- **Combat and exploration evolve into social puzzles**: The game shifts from combat-focused gameplay to relationship-building and social dynamics.
- **Return to make a difference**: The player eventually returns to the small town to solve its systemic problems and become a valued community member.

## Stats & Progression
- **Drive**: Represents energy and the player's ability to take action. It is the result of two sub-traits:
  - **Determination**: How much a player pushes forward.
  - **Patience**: How much a player can wait or handle monotony.
- **Insight**: Determines how much the player understands game mechanics and social systems. It is the result of:
  - **Wisdom**: Knowledge gained from experiences or books.
  - **Humility**: The ability to reflect and admit limitations.
- **Confidence**: Enables players to unlock social interactions and dialogue options. It is the result of:
  - **Fortitude**: Ability to handle discomfort and take risks socially.
  - **Perception**: Ability to observe and understand social dynamics.

### Relationship Stats
As players interact with NPCs, their relationship stats change based on choices:
- **Familiarity**: How comfortable the NPC is with the player.
- **Trust**: How much the NPC trusts the player.
- **Impressed**: How impressed the NPC is with the player's abilities.
- **Comfortability**: How at ease the NPC is with the player.

## Gameplay Phases
### 1. **Small Town (Tutorial)**
- **Objective**: Learn the basics of combat and dialogue from NPCs.
- **Theme**: NPCs teach the player what they know, but their knowledge is limited by ignorance and self-interest.
- **Player Growth**: Gain initial *Drive*, *Confidence*, and *Insight*.
- **Endgame**: Break free from the small town through *Drive* (determination to explore) or *Insight* (understanding systems are deeper than taught).

### 2. **The Wilds (Mid-Game)**
- **Objective**: Travel between towns, engage in combat, and explore.
- **Theme**: Master the game mechanics and increase stats. Combat becomes more varied with different enemies using unique rhythms.
- **Player Growth**: Gain combat experience, *Drive*, and *Insight*. *Confidence* stays stagnant.

### 3. **Big City (Late-Game)**
- **Objective**: Shift focus to social dynamics. Build relationships, form alliances, and help NPCs solve personal problems.
- **Theme**: The big city is about *Confidence*—navigating social hierarchies and unlocking hidden dialogue options.
- **Gameplay Shift**: Dialogue becomes more complex with a GPT-driven system. Players can input their own dialogue after a certain confidence threshold.
- **Endgame**: Gain insight into the city's social systems and realize that true progress is made through helping others, not by financial or social dominance.

### 4. **Return to the Small Town (Endgame)**
- **Objective**: Improve the small town using the player's gained knowledge and skills.
- **Theme**: Use relationships and social dynamics to solve systemic problems. The player's understanding of the town's problems allows them to make lasting changes.
- **Key Quests**:
  - **Crop Rotation**: Convince the farmers to rotate crops for better yield.
  - **Cattle Rancher**: Broker peace between the rancher and farmers.
  - **Shopkeeper**: Help the shopkeeper expand his inventory by forming trade relations.
- **Endgame Goal**: Settle down, marry a hometown NPC, and improve the town’s quality of life. Build a future for the next generation.

## Key Mechanics
- **Combat**: Rhythm-based combat where enemies queue up actions based on their unique rhythm. As players gain *Insight*, they begin to understand enemy attack patterns and system complexity.
- **Dialogue System**: Standard dialogue options evolve into open-ended GPT-driven input once the player's *Confidence* is high enough. The player’s responses affect NPC relationship stats.
- **NPC Quests**: Each NPC has a questline tied to improving their life in the town. Success depends on relationship stats and dialogue choices.
- **Town Improvement**: As the player builds relationships, they can solve systemic problems in the small town, like improving crop yields, forming trade routes, or reducing tensions between NPCs.
- **Marriage & Family**: Endgame involves forming meaningful relationships and settling down in the town. The player’s final "quest" is to balance personal happiness with community improvement.

## Visual Style & Tools
- **Art Style**: 8-bit/16-bit RPG Maker-style art, generated and touched up using tools like MidJourney and Aseprite.
- **World Maps**: Simple RPG-style maps with labeled locations like the Apothecary, Blacksmith, and various houses.
- **Technology**: Web-based game using Vue.js, TypeScript, and GPT-3.5 for open-ended dialogue interaction.

## Endgame
- **Final Objective**: Solve enough of the town's problems to become a valued member of the community. Form a relationship, marry, and create a better future for the town.
- **Post-Game Idea**: (Optional) Explore themes of fatherhood and balancing responsibilities as a family man.
