# Bad explanation

Early on, an NPC mis-explains combat.

* You have an "energy" bar (it's the combination of all three stats)
* When your "energy" hits zero, you're done.
  * When "drive" hits zero, you give up
  * When "insight" hits zero, you ...
  * When "stability" hits zero, you enter a daze and flee
* After a fight, your "energy" bar does not auomatically restore.
* It's suggested "you're pretty beat up! Looks like you could use a drink at the bar here".
* If you drink at the bar, your "energy" is restored.

## However

If you rest after drinking, the dialog says "You feel good and buzzed. You fall asleep the moment your head hits the pillow". You have no time to reflect, and you do not level up.

If you rest without drinking, you get a dialog choice.

> "You lay down but can't seem to get to sleep. You think about your day".

* [Choice] What do you choose to focus on?
  * Try to stay calm
  * Try to stay confident
  * Try to stay passionate.

You'll level up whatever skill is opposite your "focus".

* Try to stay calm
  * [First time] (calm goes down by one)
  * [Second time] (calm goes down again, but you somehow feel stronger)
  * [Third time] (paradigm shift, calm is negative, drive is positive. Drive has gone up.)

After three "dreams", the stat will flip from negative to positive.

## Calm → Drive

1. You remind yourself to stay calm. It helps, for a while.
2. You take deep breaths. The quiet stretches on, and you wonder how long you've been here.
3. You tell yourself this is fine. But maybe fine isn't what you want. Maybe you've been calling it calm so you don't have to call it stuck. **[FLIP to Drive]**

## Confidence → Insight

1. You tell yourself you know what you're doing. Even if you don't.
2. You remember your bold choices today. And the mistakes you didn't admit.
3. You've been calling it confidence. But maybe real confidence starts with humility. **[FLIP to Insight]**

## Passion → Stability

1. You throw yourself into the memory of the fight, heat still in your veins.
2. Your pulse pounds in your ears. The fire feels good… until it doesn't.
3. You've been calling it passion. But it burns too hot, too fast. Maybe it's time to stand your ground. **[FLIP to Stability]**
